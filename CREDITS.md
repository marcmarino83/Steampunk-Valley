# Third-Party Assets

- **Pine trees pack (lowpoly, game ready, LODs)** by LOLIPOP, from Sketchfab. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Used in: scattered forest trees. File: `assets/trees/pine_pack/pine_trees_pack_lowpoly_game_ready_lods.glb`.
- **Tree 3D Pack - MEGA** by ToffeeCraft (itch.io), used under its commercial license. Used for: birch, Douglas fir, maple, and willow trees. (Not included in this repository: the license does not allow redistribution.)
- **Ground textures** from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), licensed under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain): Ground037 (forest floor), Ground108 (gravel path), Rock051 (mossy and plain rock), Rock022 (cliff rock) and Rocks011 (scree), Ground049C (sandy path), Ground109 (light soil) and Ground048 (dark soil). Used for: the terrain's forest floor, trails and mountains, the Texture Brush, and the Object Painter's rocks. Files: `assets/textures/`.
- **Wrecked airship** (the crash site): model created with Meshy (https://www.meshy.ai), an AI 3D generator, then reduced to about 50,000 triangles in Blender. Used under Meshy's terms: models made on Meshy's free plan are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/; models made on a paid plan belong to their creator. File: `assets/crashed_airship/Wrecked_Airship_50k.glb`.
- **Rock outcrops** (five rock formations): models created with Meshy (https://www.meshy.ai), an AI 3D generator. Used for: large placeable rock formations. Files: `assets/rocks/outcrops/`.
- **Steampunk castle ruin**: model created with Meshy (https://www.meshy.ai), an AI 3D generator. Used for: the ruined fortress landmark. Files: `assets/landmarks/ruined_castle/`.
- **Stone tool pictures** (hatchet, hammer, pickaxe, knife, adze): images created with Meshy (https://www.meshy.ai). Used for: the tools' icons, made by cutting out the backgrounds of the first four. Files: `assets/tier_1_tools/` and `assets/tier_1_tools/icons/`.
- **Stone tool models** (axe, hammer, pickaxe, knife, each with its textures, and an adze not yet used): models created with Meshy (https://www.meshy.ai), an AI 3D generator. Used for: the tools held in your hand and dropped on the ground. Files: `assets/tier_1_tools/axe/`, `hammer/`, `pickaxe/`, `knife/` and `Meshy_AI_Tool_5_Stone_Adze_20261107_image-to-3d-texture_fbx/`.
- **Stone, sticks and plant fiber pickups** (granite stone, pine sticks, plant fiber clump: low-poly models with their textures built in): models created with Meshy (https://www.meshy.ai), an AI 3D generator. Used for: the stone, sticks and plant fiber gathered by hand around the valley. Files: `assets/rocks/stone/`, `assets/sticks/`, `assets/bushes/plant_fiber/`.
- **Campfire and lean-to**: models created with Meshy (https://www.meshy.ai), an AI 3D generator, with their textures. Used for: the campfire and lean-to the player builds. Files: `assets/buildables/campfire/`, `assets/buildables/lean_to/`.
- **Workbench and storage chest**: models created with Meshy (https://www.meshy.ai), an AI 3D generator, with their textures. Used for: the workbench and chest the player builds. Files: `assets/buildables/workbench/`, `assets/buildables/storage_chest/`.
- **Workbench and storage chest icons**: concept images generated with Google Gemini, cropped and with the background removed. Used for: the build menu's icons, and the workbench mark on workbench recipes. Files: `assets/buildables/workbench/icon.png`, `assets/buildables/storage_chest/icon.png`.
- **Campfire and lean-to icons**: concept images generated with Google Gemini, cropped and with the background removed. Used for: the build menu's icons. Files: `assets/buildables/campfire/icon.png`, `assets/buildables/lean_to/icon.png`.
- **Spear and torch**: models created with Meshy (https://www.meshy.ai), an AI 3D generator, with their textures. Used for: the spear and torch held in your hand and dropped on the ground (the torch's flame is made in the game). Files: `assets/tier_1_tools/spear/`, `assets/tier_1_tools/torch/`.
- **Item icons** (rope, planks, torch, spear, wood, stone, sticks, plant fiber, berries): concept images generated with Google Gemini, cropped and with the background removed. Used for: those items' icons in the inventory, crafting list and action bar. Files: `assets/icons/`. The other icons there (coal, ores, scrap, herbs, mushrooms, dowsing rod) are simple placeholders drawn for the game.
- **HUD and menu art** (action bar, compass with clock, vitals panel with gauge, blank plaque, ornate title frame, crosshair): images generated with Google Gemini, with the magenta background removed; pink and copper tints left by the background are turned to brass; the action bar's left scrollwork is trimmed off and the slot pictures are cropped from it; the clock's printed hands are painted out. Used for: the action bar, compass and clock, hunger and warmth panel, menu frames and title screen frame. Files: `assets/ui/` (the untouched sources are kept in `assets/ui/hd/`, outside git).
- These are used under Meshy's terms, as with the wrecked airship: models made on Meshy's free plan are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/; models made on a paid plan belong to their creator.
